## 🪄 The Perfect Image Prompt (LINE Rich Menu)
ใช้สำหรับสร้างรูปพื้นหลังที่เข้ากับเว็บไซต์ 100% ผ่าน Gemini (Nano Banana Pro)

> **Prompt**: "Create a high-quality, professional background for a LINE Rich Menu. The design concept is 'True MimiVibe' – a soft, elegant, and modern mystic aesthetic.
> 
> **Color Palette**: Use a very soft, warm pink background (#FFF0F0). Complement it with delicate accents of misty rose (#FFD6D1) and muted gold (#D4AF37). The overall look should be bright and airy.
> 
> **Visual Elements**: Incorporate subtle 'Glassmorphism' effects with soft-edged frosted glass panels (white/70% opacity). Add thin, elegant gold lines and tiny sparkling star glitters. Use organic, flowing shapes like silk or soft clouds to create depth.
> 
> **Mood**: Warm, feminine, trustworthy, and minimal. Avoid dark colors and avoid purple. The shadows should be warm maroon (#592E2E at low opacity).
> 
> **Format**: High resolution, clean layout, perfect for a tarot application interface."
