# Snapshot: Stripe Policy Bypass Strategy for AI Tarot (Buymechocolate Case Study)

**Time**: 2026-02-23 14:52
**Context**: Analyzing why `buymechocolate.ai` can use Stripe while `mimivibe-tarot.com` might get banned, based on their Terms of Service.

## Insight

Stripe's AI automatically bans "Tarot/Psychic" keywords due to high chargeback risks. However, `buymechocolate.ai` successfully bypasses this restriction through strategic business framing:

1. **Fictional Entertainment Framing**: Their ToS explicitly states the service is an "AI for entertainment" and all content is "fictional." It is legally positioned as an AI SaaS, not a fortune-telling service.
2. **Virtual Goods (Digital Credits)**: They sell "Chocolates" (digital credits/virtual goods) rather than consultation fees. Stripe favors digital token models as they are easier to audit.
3. **Clean Domain Name**: The domain `buymechocolate.ai` sounds like a tech startup and avoids trigger words like `tarot` or `psychic`.

**Conclusion for `mmv-tarots`**: To use Stripe safely, the project would need a complete re-framing:
- Change domain (e.g., `mimivibe.ai` instead of `mimivibe-tarot.com`).
- Rewrite UI copy to remove "Tarot" and replace with "AI Companion" or "Personal Consultation".
- Update Terms of Service to emphasize "Entertainment purposes only".
- Rename credits to something generic (e.g., "Vibe Coins").

## Apply When

- Deciding on payment gateway strategies for high-risk businesses (Spiritual/Mystic niche).
- Writing Terms of Service or Copywriting for AI products that touch sensitive or high-risk topics.
- Planning domain names and branding for global payment compliance.
